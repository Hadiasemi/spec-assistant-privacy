Privacy Policy

Spec Assistant · last updated 12 November 2026

Spec Assistant answers questions from published construction specifications. It is a pilot tool for construction field staff.

What the app collects

To measure whether its answers are correct, the app records each question and answer and sends them to a feedback service, together with:

  • the question text as typed, and any detail written when an answer is marked incorrect
  • whether the reader marked an answer correct or incorrect, and whether they opened the cited page
  • the passages the search returned, and which language model produced the answer
  • an install identifier, generated by the app on first launch

What the app does not collect

No name, email address, phone number or postal address. No account, because the app has none. No device identifier, advertising identifier or location. Nothing is used for tracking, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

The install identifier is created by the app and is not connected to any account or to the device's own identifiers. It exists so that several answers from one installation can be counted together, and it cannot be used to identify a person.

When data is sent

Records are written to the device first and sent afterwards, so no feedback is lost when there is no signal. They are sent over an encrypted connection to the service operated by the California Department of Transportation for this pilot, and are used to improve which sentence the app quotes.

What stays on the device

The specifications, the search index and the language model are stored on the device and are never sent anywhere. Questions are answered on the device; no question is sent to a third-party service in order to answer it.

Turning it off

Settings in the app has a switch that turns off the “Was this answer correct?” prompt. The app answers questions exactly as before with it off.

Removing data

To have the records from your installation removed, write to the address below with the install identifier shown in the app’s Settings screen.
